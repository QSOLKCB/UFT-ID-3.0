This file records the research lineage used to build UFT-ID 3.0. It is a provenance map, not a claim that every historical document is mutually consistent or currently endorsed.

## Mathematical material retained for possible reuse

### Fixed-point and self-modeling material

The AGI Mirror Stage work contains useful independent mathematics involving:

- Lawvere fixed points;
- Scott domains and directed-complete partial orders;
- Kleene fixed-point iteration;
- recursive types;
- Löbian limitations on self-certification;
- reflective probability.

UFT-ID 3.0 may reuse fixed-point and observer mathematics after stripping the AGI or consciousness interpretation from theorem statements.

### Operational field formulations

Earlier UFT-ID work explored constraint density, propagation velocity, observer coupling, collapse thresholds, and measurable proxies. These are candidates for a later mesoscopic specialization, not initial axioms.

### Representational and lattice formulations

Historical work also explored:

- ternary information logic;
- qutrit representations;
- SU(3) invariants;
- E8 and other lattice structures;
- geometry-first admissibility;
- sonification of structural diagnostics.

These are retained as candidate representations and applications. Their use does not establish that the represented domain literally possesses the chosen algebra or lattice.
