# Explicit Non-Claims

UFT-ID 3.0 deliberately refuses several conclusions that earlier exploratory material sometimes approached more aggressively.

## UFT-ID 3.0 does not currently claim

1. that information is a fifth state of matter;
2. that information has an intrinsic rest mass;
3. that Shannon entropy is a physical substance;
4. that all meaningful information measures obey one universal monotonic law;
5. that decreasing information entropy proves data compression by the universe;
6. that gravity is caused by information compression;
7. that the universe is a simulation or computer;
8. that spacetime is an E8 lattice;
9. that spacetime is necessarily a loop-quantized lattice;
10. that SU(3) is the fundamental algebra of semantic information;
11. that qutrit or ternary models are ontologically privileged;
12. that AI hallucination and quantum collapse are the same physical process;
13. that consciousness is a physical field;
14. that AGI self-modeling is inevitable merely from the existence of mathematical fixed-point theorems;
15. that sonification reveals hidden physical causation;
16. that cross-domain similarity establishes common mechanism;
17. that a deterministic recovery operator describes every real collapse or correction event;
18. that the Inference Transport Diagnostic Map is a validated universal detection method;
19. that diagnostic risk labels are universal quantitative thresholds;
20. that Lean formalization, when added, would prove a physical interpretation of the mathematics.

## Why these non-claims matter

The project explicitly adopts an abstraction non-reification rule:

```text
P(model(X)) does not imply P(X)
```

unless a bridge theorem or empirical argument establishes the connection.

A lattice can be a useful state-space model without spacetime being a lattice. An SU(3) representation can preserve useful invariants without semantic information literally carrying color charge. A coarse-graining map can reduce an observed entropy without destroying an underlying microstate distinction.

## Vopson comparison boundary

The repository may conclude that a published infodynamics claim is:

- correct under stated restrictions;
- broader than its derivation supports;
- dependent on encoding or representation;
- empirically unsupported;
- reproducible but non-unique in interpretation;
- contradicted by a valid counterexample;
- or still unresolved.

It will not convert any of those scientific conclusions into claims about the author's motives, competence, or character.
