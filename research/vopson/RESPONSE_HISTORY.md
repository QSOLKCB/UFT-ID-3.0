# Published Response and Reformulation History

This file records public scholarly responses so UFT-ID does not present an already-addressed objection as new.

## 2025 — Vopson self-critique of the cosmological route

**Work:** *On the second law of infodynamics from cosmological thermodynamics*  
**DOI:** `10.59973/ipil.137`  
**Role:** author communication / reformulation

The official metadata states that the cosmological derivation contains a point of weakness requiring further discussion. Any UFT-ID cosmology audit must therefore distinguish the original argument from the later qualification.

## 2025 — Thermocontextual reformulation

**Work:** Harrison Crecraft, *The Second Law of Infodynamics: A Thermocontextual Reformulation*  
**DOI:** `10.3390/e27010022`  
**Role:** independent reformulation

Treat this as a distinct framework, not automatic validation of every original SLI application. It may supply a narrower mechanism or information quantity.

## 2025 — Response to Hossenfelder’s gravity commentary

**Work:** Vopson, *Response to Sabine Hossenfelder’s Commentary on Vopson’s Paper: Is gravity evidence of a computational universe?*  
**DOI:** `10.59973/ipil.212`  
**Role:** author response

The response argues that the triggering video commentary did not provide a formal equation-by-equation rebuttal. UFT-ID must therefore ground its gravity criticism in the published equations and reproducible counter-calculations, not in the authority of a video.

## 2026 — Relative-entropy thermodynamic foundation

**Work:** Ian Todd, *A Thermodynamic Foundation for the Second Law of Infodynamics*  
**DOI:** `10.59973/ipil.308`  
**Role:** independent conditional foundation

The paper interprets structure-information using relative entropy to an isotropic reference and derives a thermodynamic mechanism under stated assumptions. This is relevant to a restricted UFT-ID embedding, but changing from raw Shannon entropy to a reference-dependent KL quantity must be declared explicitly.

## Editorial-context note

The University of Portsmouth profile records Vopson’s editorial role with IPI Letters. This fact is bibliographic context only. It neither invalidates nor validates papers in the venue; derivation, evidence and independent reproduction remain decisive.
