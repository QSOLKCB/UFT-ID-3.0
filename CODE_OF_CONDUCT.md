# Research Conduct

UFT-ID 3.0 welcomes hard criticism. The project is explicitly designed to survive adversarial review.

## Expected conduct

- Attack claims, equations, assumptions, data, and methods rather than people.
- Cite primary sources where practical.
- Distinguish error from disagreement and disagreement from misconduct.
- State uncertainty and limitations.
- Credit prior work accurately.
- Correct mistakes visibly.
- Welcome valid counterexamples, including those that weaken UFT-ID itself.
- Do not manipulate benchmarks, partitions, datasets, or stopping rules to manufacture a desired result.

## Unacceptable conduct

- harassment or personal abuse;
- fabricated evidence or citations;
- knowingly misleading representations of another researcher's claim;
- hiding a failed reproduction that materially changes a conclusion;
- publishing secrets, private data, or identifying information without authorization;
- presenting speculation as established fact.

## Scientific disagreement

Strong disagreement is allowed and encouraged when it is specific and evidenced. A contribution may say that a theorem is false, a derivation is circular, a dataset does not support a conclusion, or an interpretation is non-unique. It should show why.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove contributions that violate these standards. Technical and scientific criticism will not be removed merely because it is uncomfortable or strongly worded, provided it remains focused on the work.
